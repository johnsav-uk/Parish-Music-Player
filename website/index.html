Music for the service, at the press of one button

Hymns, MIDI and recorded music in one playlist, played and faded from a single button or a presentation clicker. Made so that whoever is on the rota this Sunday can run the music without a second thought.

Two ways to use it

The same player either way. Choose whichever suits your church.

With internet

Play online

Open it in your browser and load your music straight away. Nothing to install.

  • Works in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on a computer, tablet or phone
  • Your music files stay on your computer. They are never uploaded
  • Settings are remembered in that browser
  • The instruments download the first time (about 55 MB), so try it before the service, and on Wi-Fi if you are using a phone

No internet in church

Install on Windows

Install it once while you have a connection, then it runs entirely offline.

  • Windows 10 or 11
  • Everything is included: all nineteen instruments and the pipe organ
  • Runs in its own window, starting from the Start menu
  • Settings kept between services
Download the installer

Latest version from GitHub.

On your phone? The installer is for the church’s Windows computer. Send yourself the link to open there.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

The installer is not signed with a paid commercial certificate, so Windows is cautious about it. Click More info, then Run anyway. If Windows says it “cannot access the specified device, path or file”, right-click the installer, choose Properties, tick Unblock and click OK. On a managed PC, ask whoever looks after it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Load the service

    Drag in your hymns and recordings. Number the files 01, 02… and they line up in order.

  2. 2

    Choose the sound

    Pick an instrument for each MIDI hymn: pipe organ for the entrance, piano or harp for Communion.

  3. 3

    Press to play

    One press plays. Press twice or hold to fade. The next track is ready and waiting.

Controls
ActionButtonKeyboardClicker
Play the next trackPressSpace, Right arrow, EnterForward
Fade out and stopPress twice, or holdLeft arrow, F, or hold SpaceBack
Stop at once—Escape—

A single accidental press while music is playing does nothing, so a knocked clicker will not interrupt the service.

  • Audio and MIDI togetherMP3, WAV, OGG and FLAC alongside MIDI in one playlist.
  • A real pipe organSampled pipe by pipe from the 1967 Hammarberg organ in Bureå church.
  • Level matchingRecordings and MIDI are balanced against each other, so nothing jumps out.
  • Tempo on the flySlow a hymn down or speed it up while it plays, without changing its pitch.
  • Remembers each hymnIts tempo and instrument come back next time the file is loaded.
  • Easy to readWhite, Parchment or Blue colour schemes, and layouts for any size of screen.

Free hymn music to download

These sites offer MIDI files, and some MP3 recordings, of hymns that you can download free and load straight into the player.

  • Hymnary.org The largest hymn index online. Many tune pages carry a MIDI file to download. MIDI · Sheet music
  • The Cyber Hymnal Thousands of hymns, each with its words, history and a MIDI of the tune. MIDI · Words
  • NetHymnal A free hymn collection with words and MIDI tunes, arranged alphabetically. MIDI · Words
  • Small Church Music Hymns played on pipe organ and piano, free for churches without a musician. MP3 · MIDI
  • The Open Hymnal Project Public-domain hymns with MIDI files, free to use and share. MIDI · Public domain
  • HymnSite.com MIDI files for the hymns of a full hymnal, listed by title and number. MIDI
  • Hymns and Carols of Christmas Advent and Christmas carols with their history, many with MIDI. MIDI · Seasonal
  • The Mutopia Project Free classical scores with MIDI, including organ voluntaries and chorales. MIDI · Organ

These are independent sites, not connected with Parish Music Player. Many hymn tunes are in the public domain, but some arrangements and recordings are not; check each site’s terms, and your church’s copyright licence, before use.
