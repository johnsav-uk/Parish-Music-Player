Appearance

White is the plainest and the easiest to read. Parchment is the same scheme on a warmer, paper-coloured ground. Blue is the original, and is gentlest in a dark church.
Wide puts the service list beside the Play button and fits a whole service on one screen. Anchored is the same, with the sound controls and one full-width Play button fixed to the bottom of the window, so only the list ever scrolls. Tall is the single narrow column, which suits a small or portrait monitor.
